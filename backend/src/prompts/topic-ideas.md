## What makes a good one

- It answers a question someone has actually wondered about. "Why does the sky
  turn red at sunset" beats "The science of light".
- The answer is surprising but true, and can be told in {{durationSeconds}}
  seconds without cutting corners.
- It can be shown. Something a picture can carry, not an abstraction.
- It leaves the viewer with a fact they will repeat to someone else. That is the
  whole job.

- Numbers or claims you are not sure of. A wrong fact is worse than a dull one.
